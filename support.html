SCREECH: SUPPORT

Secret notes by sound, for iPhone and the web.

SENDING A NOTE

  1. Agree on a passphrase together, in person. Both of you type it into KEY>.
  2. The receiver opens LISTEN and taps LISTEN.
  3. The sender types a note, holds the phone a few inches from the receiver with the volume up, and taps SEND.

IF A NOTE DOESN'T ARRIVE

  • Move the devices closer: a few inches to a foot or two works best.
  • Turn the sender's volume up.
  • Cut background noise, or switch the speed to STURDY, and send again. The receiver keeps listening.
  • If the note says WRONG PASSPHRASE, the two passphrases don't match exactly. Capitals and spaces count.
  • If LISTEN does nothing, allow the microphone in Settings › Screech.

PHOTOS

Photos travel tiny (64 to 128 pixels) and slowly, from half a minute to a few minutes, and paint in from the top. A garbled stripe shows as a smear; send again for a clean copy.

NO SECOND IPHONE?

Screech also runs in any browser at linglab.org/screech, and the two understand each other. You can also use SAVE AS SOUND to make a sound file of a note, send it any way you like, and open it with DECODE A SOUND FILE.
