SCREECH: PRIVACY

Applies to the Screech iPhone app and the web version at linglab.org/screech. Last updated November 24, 2026.

THE SHORT VERSION

Screech collects nothing. There is no account, no server, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. Everything Screech does happens on your own device.

MICROPHONE

Screech uses the microphone only while you have LISTEN switched on, to hear notes played by another device. The sound is analysed on your device as it arrives and then discarded. It is never recorded, saved or sent anywhere.

NOTES AND PHOTOS

  • Notes and photos you send leave your device only as sound from its speaker, or as a sound file if you choose SAVE AS SOUND and share it yourself.
  • Notes and photos you receive are held in memory and are gone when you close Screech. A received photo is saved to your photo library only if you choose to save it.
  • When you pick a photo to send, Screech sees only the photo you picked, and shrinks it on your device.

PASSPHRASE

Your passphrase is never transmitted, not even inside a note. It is used on your device to make the encryption key. It is stored only if you switch on REMEMBER: on iPhone in the device's Keychain (this device only, not synced), and in the web version in that browser's local storage. Switch REMEMBER off and it is erased.

THE WEB VERSION

The web version is a set of static files served by GitHub Pages, and it works offline once loaded. It sets no cookies and makes no requests of its own. As with any website, the host (GitHub) may log the IP address of visitors; see GitHub's privacy statement.

CHILDREN

Screech collects no personal information from anyone, including children.

CHANGES AND QUESTIONS

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page. Questions: support.
